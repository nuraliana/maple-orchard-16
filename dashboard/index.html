Ablation — every factor must earn its place on all three legs (geometric factor-miss, lower is better)

ModelLeave-one-outTime-splitWalk-forwardDecision

Honest weak legs: age loses to a no-age model on all three legs (3.510 / 3.264 / 3.172 vs 3.547 / 3.433 / 3.472) — age transforms (floor, cap 365/730, log1p, sqrt, linear) were tried and none beat no-age on all three — so the value model now uses no age at all. Offsets lose to dropping them on LOO (3.478 vs 3.510) and time-split (3.188 vs 3.264) and on the 3-leg mean (3.281 vs 3.315), trailing only on walk-forward by 0.005 (3.177 vs 3.172), so the final model uses no offsets either. Growth w=0.3 leaves LOO unchanged and worsens the other two legs on the final basis, so the weight is 0. Log chains worsens all three legs on the final basis, so it is dropped. The model still beats both baselines on all three legs.
